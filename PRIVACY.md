## 1. INTRODUCTION

Pablo Offline AI LLC ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our software—Pablo Enterprise (Windows) and Pablo Offline AI (Android)—handles your information.

**Our core principle: Your data never leaves your devices.**

---

## 2. DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT

Unlike cloud-based AI services, we do NOT collect:

- ❌ Your conversations with the AI
- ❌ Your queries or prompts
- ❌ AI-generated responses
- ❌ Personal information (name, email, location)
- ❌ Usage analytics or telemetry
- ❌ Device identifiers
- ❌ IP addresses
- ❌ Browsing history
- ❌ Contacts, photos, or files
- ❌ Any data whatsoever

**We have no servers. We have no database of user information. We cannot access your data because it never reaches us.**

---

## 3. HOW THE SOFTWARE WORKS

### 3.1 Local Processing

All AI processing occurs **entirely on your device**:

- AI models run locally on your computer/phone
- Conversations are processed locally
- No internet connection is required for core functionality
- No data is sent to external servers

### 3.2 Local Data Storage

The Software stores data only on your local device:

- **Pablo Enterprise (Windows):** Data stored in `%LocalAppData%\PabloEnterprise\`
- **Pablo Offline AI (Android):** Data stored in app-private storage

This local data may include:
- Your conversation history (if enabled)
- App settings and preferences
- Downloaded AI models
- Knowledge database

**This data never leaves your device unless you manually export it.**

---

## 4. OPTIONAL FEATURES THAT MAY USE NETWORK

Some optional features require network connectivity:

### 4.1 Model Downloads

When you download AI models, the Software connects to:
- HuggingFace (huggingface.co) to download model files

These downloads do not transmit any personal data. Only standard HTTP requests are made.

### 4.2 Tailscale Integration (Optional)

If you enable Tailscale VPN for remote access:
- Tailscale handles the VPN connection
- Pablo communicates only within your private Tailscale network
- See Tailscale's privacy policy for their data practices

### 4.3 News/Weather Features (Android, Optional)

If you enable news or weather features:
- RSS feeds are fetched from public news sources
- Weather data is fetched from weather APIs
- **Your location is never transmitted** unless you explicitly provide coordinates

### 4.4 Google Calendar Integration (Optional)
If you connect your Google Calendar through the Android app:

- Pablo uses the calendar.readonly scope to read your event titles, times, and descriptions
- Calendar data is cached locally on your device and your personal Pablo server only
- Calendar data is NEVER transmitted to Pablo Offline AI LLC or any third party
- You can revoke access at any time via https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

## 9. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

### 9.1 We Retain Nothing

We do not retain any user data because we do not collect any.

### 9.2 Local Data Deletion

To delete data stored locally on your device:

**Windows:**
1. Uninstall Pablo Enterprise
2. Delete: `%LocalAppData%\PabloEnterprise\`

**Android:**
1. Uninstall Pablo Offline AI
2. All app data is automatically deleted
