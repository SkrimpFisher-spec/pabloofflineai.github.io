Security First

Built to stay private

Encryption, no spying, and an app that keeps working even if we disappear tomorrow.

Encrypted at Rest

Your conversations, memories, and personal data are encrypted on disk using military-grade AES-256 encryption. Even if someone copies your files, they can't read them. The encryption key is tied to your Windows account and can't be used by anyone else.

Zero Data Collection

No tracking. No analytics. No accounts. We do not run servers that store your chats, which means there is nothing to hack, nothing to subpoena, and nothing to sell. We literally cannot see your data.

Yours Forever

Pablo runs on your computer with no dependency on our company. If we shut down tomorrow, your AI keeps working. No license server to go dark, no subscription to expire, no update that bricks your install. Once it is yours, you own your access to AI.

Verified Downloads

Every AI model and system component Pablo downloads is verified with a cryptographic checksum before it runs. If a file has been tampered with in transit, Pablo rejects it. No backdoors. No compromised binaries.

When You Say Forget, It's Gone

Tell Pablo to forget something and it's permanently erased from your computer, not just hidden. The data is wiped and the freed space is overwritten so it can't be recovered, even with forensic tools. No cloud backup holding onto it. Your delete means delete.

Works Without Internet

Once installed, Pablo runs without internet. Chat, memory, and smart home control all work offline. Calendar and weather need a connection to refresh, but the AI itself never requires the cloud.

No Account Required

No email, no password, no sign-up. Install and use. Pablo Offline AI LLC doesn't know who you are and doesn't need to. There's no user profile on any server because there is no server.

No Training on Your Data

Your conversations are never uploaded, analyzed, or fed into model training. Unlike cloud AI services that improve their models using what you type, Pablo's AI runs locally and your words stay your words. Nothing you say makes someone else's product better.

Questions

What is Pablo Offline AI?

Pablo is AI chat for Windows that runs on your computer instead of on someone else's servers. You type questions and get answers—like popular online chat services—but your words stay on your PC. Pablo can also remember context, search your files, show briefings, control smart-home devices, and connect to a free Android app on your phone. No account required. The installer is free; a one-time license unlocks the full desktop app. The Android companion is free. No subscription.

What are expert modes?

Pablo can use different AI models for different jobs—everyday chat, coding, understanding photos, wellness, creative writing, and more. Instead of one generic chatbot for everything, Pablo sends each question to the mode that fits best.

Does Pablo work without internet?

Yes. Once installed, Pablo runs completely offline. Conversations, smart home control, and reminders all work without internet. Calendar and weather features require a connection to sync data, but the AI itself never needs the cloud.

Can Pablo control my smart home?

Pablo integrates with TP-Link Kasa smart home devices. Say "turn on the living room lights" and Pablo handles it on your home network. More smart home brands are planned.

How is Pablo different from online AI chat?

Online AI chat runs on company servers—you send your words out, they send answers back. Pablo runs on your Windows PC. The conversation never has to leave your house. You are not renting access month to month, and your chats are not used to train someone else's product. Pablo also does more than chat: documents, briefings, smart home, and a phone companion that talks to your PC.

What AI model does Pablo use?

Pablo does not ship one locked-in AI. You choose compatible models sized to your computer or graphics card. The default install includes Phi-3.5-mini to get started quickly. Use a code model for programming, a vision model for photos, and swap anytime.

Do I need a powerful GPU?

No. Pablo figures out your computer automatically—NVIDIA graphics card (fastest), Intel, or CPU-only. A dedicated NVIDIA card with plenty of graphics memory gives the best experience, but Pablo runs on any modern Windows PC.

Is Pablo free?

Pablo Desktop requires a one-time $14.99 license, purchased at pablooffline.com/buy.html. The download and Android companion app are both free. No subscription. No ads, ever.
