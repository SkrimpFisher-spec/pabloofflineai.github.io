How it works

Pablo runs on your PC.
Everything else runs on Pablo.

Most AI lives in a company's data center, and every app or gadget that wants to be “smart” has to call out to it. Pablo turns that around. The thinking happens on the computer you already own, and everything else in your life can lean on it.

  1. You install Pablo on your PC.

    That is where the real work happens: the AI, your memories, your documents, and your settings. Nothing is sent to a company server.

  2. Pablo stays ready at home.

    While your PC is on and Pablo is running, it is there for you and for anything you connect to it, whether you are at your desk, on the couch, or out running errands.

  3. Other things run on Pablo.

    Your phone, your widgets, and your lights do not need AI built into them. They ask the Pablo on your PC, so they all share the same memory and the same smarts.

Your PC, running Pablodoes the thinking and keeps your memories. From there, it powers:

  • Pablo Mobile, the free Android app

    Chat with your Pablo from your phone, at home or away. Your phone is not a second AI in the cloud. It is a window into the one on your desk.

  • Home-screen widgets

    Weather, calendar, news, and quick commands on your Android home screen. Glance down and you are caught up.

  • Daily briefings

    Weather, news from sources you pick, and your upcoming calendar, put together by Pablo and ready on your desktop and your phone.

  • Smart lights and plugs

    Say “turn off the living room lights” and it happens on your home network. TP-Link Kasa today, with more brands on the way.

  • More on the way

    We are building more things that run on Pablo. When they arrive, they will connect to the Pablo you already have.

What Pablo can do

One assistant, plenty to do.

The same privacy promise throughout: your words stay on your computer, not on a company server.

It remembers the plot.

Most AI forgets you five minutes later. Pablo remembers what matters, checks facts before guessing, and keeps answers tied to your life, not a generic internet average, no matter which AI model you choose.

Everything in one conversation.

Calendar, weather, smart home, documents, and briefings can all feed the same chat without dumping your whole life into every reply. Focus on one file, and Pablo keeps the rest out of the way.

Memory you can watch grow.

Pablo stores what you teach it as connected memories, ideas linked together, so recall feels natural instead of like a keyword search. Your memories are encrypted on your PC, and when you tell Pablo to forget something, it is deleted.

We call this structured on-device memory a “cortex.” It is software architecture, not a claim of sentience.

The right AI for each job.

Use one AI for photos, another for code, another for long writing. Pablo works with many AI models, picks the one that fits each question, and respects what your computer can handle.

A built-in fact library.

Briefings that meet you at the door.

Private by design

Built to stay private.

Encryption, no spying, and an app that keeps working even if we disappear tomorrow.

Encrypted at rest

Your conversations, memories, and personal data are encrypted on disk using AES-256 encryption. Even if someone copies your files, they can't read them. The encryption key is tied to your Windows account and can't be used by anyone else.

Zero data collection

No tracking. No analytics. No accounts. We do not run servers that store your chats, which means there is nothing to hack, nothing to subpoena, and nothing to sell. We literally cannot see your data.

Yours forever

Pablo runs on your computer with no dependency on our company. If we shut down tomorrow, your AI keeps working. No license server to go dark, no subscription to expire, no update that bricks your install.

Verified downloads

Every AI model and system component Pablo downloads is verified with a cryptographic checksum before it runs. If a file has been tampered with in transit, Pablo rejects it.

When you say forget, it's gone

Tell Pablo to forget something and it's permanently erased from your computer, not just hidden. The data is wiped and the freed space is overwritten so it can't be recovered, even with forensic tools. No cloud backup holding onto it.

Works without internet

Once installed, Pablo runs without internet. Chat, memory, and smart home control all work offline. Calendar and weather need a connection to refresh, but the AI itself never requires the cloud.

No account required

No email, no password, no sign-up. Install and use. Pablo Offline AI LLC doesn't know who you are and doesn't need to. There's no user profile on any server because there is no server.

No training on your data

Your conversations are never uploaded, analyzed, or fed into model training. Pablo's AI runs locally and your words stay your words. Nothing you say makes someone else's product better.

Questions

Good questions.

What is Pablo Offline AI?

Pablo is AI chat for Windows that runs on your computer instead of on someone else's servers. You type questions and get answers—like popular online chat services—but your words stay on your PC. Pablo can also remember context, search your files, show briefings, control smart-home devices, and connect to a free Android app on your phone. No account required. The installer is free; a one-time license unlocks the full desktop app. The Android companion is free. No subscription.

What are expert modes?

Pablo can use different AI models for different jobs—everyday chat, coding, understanding photos, wellness, creative writing, and more. Instead of one generic chatbot for everything, Pablo sends each question to the mode that fits best.

Does Pablo work without internet?

Yes. Once installed, Pablo runs completely offline. Conversations, smart home control, and reminders all work without internet. Calendar and weather features require a connection to sync data, but the AI itself never needs the cloud.

Can Pablo control my smart home?

Pablo integrates with TP-Link Kasa smart home devices. Say "turn on the living room lights" and Pablo handles it on your home network. More smart home brands are planned.

How is Pablo different from online AI chat?

Online AI chat runs on company servers—you send your words out, they send answers back. Pablo runs on your Windows PC. The conversation never has to leave your house. You are not renting access month to month, and your chats are not used to train someone else's product. Pablo also does more than chat: documents, briefings, smart home, and a phone companion that talks to your PC.

What AI model does Pablo use?

Pablo does not ship one locked-in AI. You choose compatible models sized to your computer or graphics card. The default install includes Phi-3.5-mini to get started quickly. Use a code model for programming, a vision model for photos, and swap anytime.

Do I need a powerful GPU?

No. Pablo figures out your computer automatically—NVIDIA graphics card (fastest), Intel, or CPU-only. A dedicated NVIDIA card with plenty of graphics memory gives the best experience, but Pablo runs on any modern Windows PC.

Is Pablo free?

Pablo Desktop requires a one-time $14.99 license, purchased at pablooffline.com/buy.html. The download and Android companion app are both free. No subscription. No ads, ever.
