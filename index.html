Security First

Built to stay private

Encryption, zero telemetry, and installs that keep working without our servers.

Encrypted at Rest

Your conversations, memories, and personal data are encrypted on disk using military-grade AES-256 encryption. Even if someone copies your files, they can't read them. The encryption key is tied to your Windows account and can't be used by anyone else.

Zero Data Collection

No telemetry. No analytics. No accounts. No tracking. We don't run servers that store your information, which means there's nothing to hack, nothing to subpoena, and nothing to sell. We literally can't see your data.

Yours Forever. No Kill Switch.

Pablo runs on your hardware with no dependency on our company. If we shut down tomorrow, your AI keeps working. No license server to go dark, no subscription to expire, no update that bricks your install. Once it's yours, you own your access to AI. That also matters when cloud policies change, APIs are deprecated, or subscriptions shift under your feet: software you install and run locally stays on your terms.

Verified Downloads

Every AI model and system component Pablo downloads is verified with a cryptographic checksum before it runs. If a file has been tampered with in transit, Pablo rejects it. No backdoors. No compromised binaries.

When You Say Forget, It's Gone

Tell Pablo to forget something and it's permanently erased from your computer, not just hidden. The data is wiped and the freed space is overwritten so it can't be recovered, even with forensic tools. No cloud backup holding onto it. Your delete means delete.

Works Without Internet

Once installed, Pablo runs entirely offline. No internet required for conversations, memory, or smart home control. Disconnect completely and it still works. Perfect for air-gapped environments or anyone who simply doesn't want software phoning home.

No Account Required

No email, no password, no sign-up. Install and use. Pablo Offline AI LLC doesn't know who you are and doesn't need to. There's no user profile on any server because there is no server.

No Training on Your Data

Your conversations are never uploaded, analyzed, or fed into model training. Unlike cloud AI services that improve their models using what you type, Pablo's AI runs locally and your words stay your words. Nothing you say makes someone else's product better.

Questions

What is Pablo Offline AI?

Pablo is a private AI assistant that runs entirely on your Windows PC. It uses local models, routes questions to specialist roles, stores encrypted memory on disk, and can ground answers with a bundled knowledge graph and your own documents. Unlike cloud AI services like ChatGPT or Claude, Pablo requires no account for the core runtime, and there is no subscription after the one-time mobile unlock.

What are specialists?

Specialists are purpose-tuned AI backends (for example Core, Vision, Coder, Wellness, Creative, Summarizer, and Curator). Pablo's orchestrator picks a path based on what you ask, instead of treating every prompt like a single generic chatbot. You configure which specialists to keep ready; others can load when needed.

Does Pablo work without internet?

Yes. Once installed, Pablo runs completely offline. Conversations, smart home control, and reminders all work without internet. Calendar and weather features require a connection to sync data, but the AI itself never needs the cloud.

Can Pablo control my smart home?

Pablo integrates with TP-Link Kasa smart home devices for light and thermostat control using natural language. Say "turn on the living room lights" and Pablo handles it locally on your network. Additional smart home platforms are planned.

How is Pablo different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT runs on OpenAI's cloud servers and requires internet. Pablo runs on your own PC. Your conversations are not uploaded for training. Pablo adds routed specialists, on-device memory, local RAG over a large entity store and your files, briefings, optional Google Calendar, smart home control on your LAN, Android widgets, and a local HTTP API — a full private stack, not just a chat box.

What AI model does Pablo use?

Any GGUF-compatible weights you choose, sized to your CPU or GPU. The default install includes Phi-3.5-mini (3.8B) for a quick start. You can assign different models per specialist (for example a code-tuned model for Coder and a vision-capable build for Vision) and swap at any time.

Do I need a powerful GPU?

No. Pablo auto-detects your hardware and configures for NVIDIA GPU acceleration (fastest), Intel with Vulkan, or CPU-only mode. A dedicated NVIDIA GPU with 4+ GB VRAM gives the best experience, but Pablo runs on any modern Windows PC.

Is Pablo really free?

For now, no: the full product requires a one-time purchase of Pablo Mobile on Google Play ($14.99). That unlocks the intended desktop + Android experience. There is no subscription and no extra in-app purchases after that. This may change in the future.
