Our Story

If you asked us to describe our relationship in one word, it would be team.

From the beginning, we both had the feeling we'd found the person we wanted to spend the rest of our lives with. What started as dating quickly became building a life together filled with shared traditions and adventures. We've spent weekends running themed 5K races, camping beneath the stars at Falling Waters State Park, dancing at an EDM music festival, and exploring anywhere that sounded like it might become our next adventure. We get tacos together on Tuesdays, but every day together feels like taco Tuesday.

Not every adventure has gone exactly as planned. On Jenni's 29th birthday, James confidently suggested a bike ride through Wakulla Springs State Park. What he didn't do was check the trail map before leading them seven miles into the park. Although they eventually made their way back, Jenni now handles the navigation.

At home, we're happiest creating things together. After a tornado forced us out of our house and we started an extended rebuild, returning home became an opportunity to start fresh. Together we chose every detail, from light fixtures to cozy rugs throughout the house. Outside, we've built a garden that has become one of our favorite places to spend time planting, growing, and caring for nature together.

That love of nature led us to one of our favorite memories: James proposing to Jenni at Cascades Park in Tallahassee, beside the waterfall, while a violinist played Jenni's favorite artist. It was a beautiful moment, but it simply made official what we had both felt from the beginning.

Today our family includes Clover the dog, and a lifetime of traditions we're still creating together. Through celebrations and setbacks, gardens and road trips, race finish lines and getting wonderfully lost, we've discovered that wherever life takes us, we're happiest when we're side by side.

We can't wait to celebrate the next chapter with all of you.

FAQ

Can I bring children?
We’re keeping this celebration to older guests so everyone can enjoy a relaxed evening together. We hope you understand and can make it.
Can I bring a plus one?
All invited guests are welcome to bring a +1. If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us directly.
Where do I park?
Free parking is available at the venue.
Is there a hotel block?
We don’t have a room block. The Glade recommends hotels in Thomasville, Bainbridge, and Tallahassee, plus cabins and rental homes closer to Cairo. See lodging details on The Glade’s lodging page.
Can I arrive early?
Guests can arrive at 3:00 PM; the ceremony begins at 3:30 PM.
Will there be gluten-free and vegan options?
We are happy to offer gluten-free and vegan options.
Will alcohol be served?
Yes. Beer, wine, and signature cocktails will be available during the reception.
Can I take pictures?
Yes, guests may take photos. After the ceremony, this website will be where guests can upload and share their photos.
