Questions

FAQ

Can I bring children?
We love your little ones, but we have chosen an adults-only celebration so everyone can relax and enjoy the evening.
Can I bring a plus one?
Your invitation will indicate if a guest is included. If you have questions, please reach out to us directly.
Where do I park?
Free parking is available at both the ceremony and reception venues. Valet will be provided at the reception.
Is there a hotel block?
Yes. We have reserved a room block at a nearby hotel. Details will be included with your invitation.
Can I arrive early?
Please plan to arrive by 3:15 PM for welcome drinks. The ceremony begins at 3:30 PM.
What if I have allergies?
Please note any dietary restrictions in your RSVP. Our caterer can accommodate most needs with advance notice.
Will alcohol be served?
Yes. Beer, wine, and cocktails will be available during the reception.
Can I take pictures?
We ask for an unplugged ceremony. Afterward, snap away and share with us using our wedding hashtag.
