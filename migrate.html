Google Play to desktop license

If you previously purchased Pablo Mobile on Google Play and need a Pablo Desktop license key under the new model, we can help manually after verification.

  1. Open your Google payments or Play order history and locate your order ID.
  2. Email Contact@PabloOffline.com with the subject line Play migration. Include your order ID and the Google account email used for the purchase.
  3. After we verify the order in Google Play Console, we will issue a desktop license key you can paste into Pablo on Windows.

New purchases use buy.html (Stripe) and receive a key automatically after payment.
