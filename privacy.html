SUMMARY

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

1. INTRODUCTION

Pablo Offline AI LLC ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our software—Pablo Enterprise (Windows) and Pablo Offline AI (Android)—handles your information.

Our core principle: Your data never leaves your devices.

2. DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT

Unlike cloud-based AI services, we do NOT collect:

  • Your conversations with the AI
  • Your queries or prompts
  • AI-generated responses
  • Personal information (name, email, location)
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Device identifiers
  • IP addresses
  • Browsing history
  • Contacts, photos, or files
  • Any data whatsoever

We have no servers. We have no database of user information. We cannot access your data because it never reaches us.

3. HOW THE SOFTWARE WORKS

3.1 Local Processing

All AI processing occurs entirely on your device:

  • AI models run locally on your computer/phone
  • Conversations are processed locally
  • No internet connection is required for core functionality
  • No data is sent to external servers

3.2 Local Data Storage

The Software stores data only on your local device:

  • Pablo Enterprise (Windows): Data stored in %LocalAppData%\PabloEnterprise\
  • Pablo Offline AI (Android): Data stored in app-private storage

This local data may include:

  • Your conversation history (if enabled)
  • App settings and preferences
  • Downloaded AI models
  • Knowledge database

This data never leaves your device unless you manually export it.

4. OPTIONAL FEATURES THAT MAY USE NETWORK

Some optional features require network connectivity:

4.1 Model Downloads

When you download AI models, the Software connects to:

  • HuggingFace (huggingface.co) to download model files

These downloads do not transmit any personal data. Only standard HTTP requests are made.

4.2 Tailscale Integration (Optional)

If you enable Tailscale VPN for remote access:

  • Tailscale handles the VPN connection
  • Pablo communicates only within your private Tailscale network
  • See Tailscale's privacy policy for their data practices

4.3 News/Weather Features (Android, Optional)

If you enable news or weather features:

  • RSS feeds are fetched from public news sources
  • Weather data is fetched from weather APIs
  • Your location is never automatically detected -- Pablo does not use GPS. If you manually enter coordinates via the location picker, those coordinates are sent directly from your device to the weather API (OpenWeatherMap) to retrieve local weather. Pablo Offline AI LLC never sees or stores your location.

4.4 Google Calendar Integration (Optional)

If you connect your Google Calendar through the Android app:

4.4 Google Calendar Integration (Optional)

If you connect your Google Calendar through the Pablo Offline AI Android app, the following applies:

Data Accessed: Pablo requests read-only access to your Google Calendar using the calendar.readonly OAuth scope. This includes event titles, dates, times, descriptions, and calendar metadata. Pablo does not request write access and cannot modify your calendar.

Data Usage: Calendar data is used solely to display your upcoming events in the Pablo briefing screen on your device. It is not analyzed, profiled, or processed for any purpose other than displaying your schedule to you.

Data Sharing: Your Google Calendar data is never shared with Pablo Offline AI LLC, any third party, any advertising service, or any external server. Calendar data travels directly from Google's servers to your device. We do not operate any servers that receive, relay, or process your calendar data.

Data Storage & Protection: Calendar data is cached locally on your device and, if you use the optional Tailscale connection, on your personal Pablo Enterprise desktop server within your private network. No calendar data is stored on any server operated by Pablo Offline AI LLC. Local storage on Android uses the app's private storage directory, which is sandboxed by the Android operating system and inaccessible to other apps.

Data Retention & Deletion: Cached calendar data is refreshed each time the app syncs and is not retained beyond what is needed to display your current schedule. All cached calendar data is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app or clear app data. You can also revoke Pablo's access to your Google Calendar at any time by visiting https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops all further access.

5. WHAT WE CANNOT DO

Because all processing is local:

  • We cannot read your conversations
  • We cannot see what you ask the AI
  • We cannot access your files
  • We cannot track your usage
  • We cannot sell your data (we don't have it)
  • We cannot respond to law enforcement requests for your data (we don't have it)
  • We cannot recover your data if you lose it

6. GOOGLE PLAY BILLING (Android Only)

If you purchase the Android app through Google Play:

  • Google handles the payment transaction
  • We receive a purchase token (a random identifier)
  • We do NOT receive your name, email, or payment details
  • See Google's privacy policy for their data practices

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our Software is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

8. DATA SECURITY

8.1 Your Responsibility

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you are responsible for:

  • Securing your device with passwords/biometrics
  • Backing up your data
  • Protecting your device from malware
  • Physical security of your device

8.2 Our Approach

We minimize security risks by:

  • Not collecting data (can't breach what we don't have)
  • Processing everything locally
  • Not requiring accounts or logins
  • Not storing anything in the cloud

9. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

9.1 We Retain Nothing

We do not retain any user data because we do not collect any.

9.2 Local Data Deletion

To delete data stored locally on your device:

Windows:

  1. Uninstall Pablo Enterprise
  2. Delete: %LocalAppData%\PabloEnterprise\

Android:

  1. Uninstall Pablo Offline AI
  2. All app data is automatically deleted
