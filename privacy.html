PRIVACY POLICY — PABLO DESKTOP AND PABLO MOBILE

Copyright (c) 2025–2026 Pablo Offline AI LLC

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

SUMMARY

  • Your AI runs on your own PC. Your conversations, prompts, AI responses, memories and files are not sent to Pablo Offline AI LLC. We have no account system and no copy of them.
  • No analytics, ads, tracking or telemetry. The apps do not send usage statistics or crash reports to us.
  • The only personal data we hold is what you give us when you buy a license (your email address and order references) or email us.
  • Optional features contact third-party services (weather, news, calendar, music, model downloads and others) only when you turn them on. They are listed in section 6.

1. WHO WE ARE

Pablo Offline AI LLC (“we,” “us”) makes Pablo Desktop (Windows) and Pablo Mobile (Android). For the limited personal data described in section 4 we are the data controller. Contact: PabloOfflineAI@proton.me.

2. HOW THE SOFTWARE WORKS

2.1 Pablo Desktop (Windows)

Pablo Desktop runs AI models on your Windows PC. Chat, memory, documents, smart-home control and briefings are processed there. We do not host your AI in a cloud.

2.2 Pablo Mobile (Android)

Pablo Mobile is a companion app. It talks to your own Pablo Desktop over your home network or a VPN you set up (for example Tailscale). Requests go to your PC, not to our servers. Pablo Mobile does not use GPS or read your contacts, photos or files.

2.3 Where your data is stored

  • Pablo Desktop: in the folder you install Pablo into (by default Documents\Pablo Desktop) and in %LocalAppData%\PabloEnterprise\. Databases holding conversations and memory are encrypted at rest with a key protected by Windows for your user account. Saved sign-in tokens for optional services are stored encrypted on your PC.
  • Pablo Mobile: in Android app-private storage (settings, the address of your PC, cached briefings, and license state).

3. WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT

We do not collect, receive or store: your conversations, prompts or AI responses; your memories, documents or files; your calendar, music or smart-home data; your location; device identifiers; usage analytics, telemetry or crash reports; or browsing history. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

4. DATA WE DO PROCESS

4.1 Buying a Pablo Desktop license

Payments on our website are processed by Stripe. After payment, our license service (hosted on Cloudflare) receives from Stripe the checkout session ID, payment reference and the email address you gave at checkout, issues your license key, and stores a record of the license ID, key, issue date, those payment references and your email. We use this only to deliver your license, help you if you lose your key, handle refunds and disputes, and meet tax and accounting obligations. We keep these records for as long as the license is supported and as required by tax law (typically up to 7 years). Legal basis (GDPR): performance of a contract and legal obligation.

License activation in Pablo Desktop happens offline: the app checks the key’s signature on your PC and does not contact us.

4.2 Pablo Mobile purchases on Google Play

Google processes any Google Play payment. The app stores the purchase token and derived license state on your device only. We do not receive your name, email or card details from Google Play purchases.

4.3 Email to us

If you email us, we use your email address and message to reply, and keep the correspondence as long as needed to support you. Legal basis: legitimate interest in answering you.

5. OPTIONAL FEATURES THAT USE THE INTERNET

The features below work only after you turn them on. When used, your PC (or phone) connects directly to the service named; these requests do not pass through our servers, and every service sees your IP address as part of a normal internet request. Your chat content is not sent to them, except that the topic of a look-up you ask for (for example a Wikipedia search) is sent to that service.

  • Model and backend downloads from Hugging Face, GitHub and NVIDIA.
  • Weather from OpenWeatherMap using your own API key and the city or ZIP code you enter; tropical storm data from the US National Hurricane Center.
  • News from the RSS feeds you enable, fetched directly from each publisher.
  • Knowledge look-ups from Wikipedia; music release information from MusicBrainz; game news from Steam.
  • Google Calendar (read-only), if you sign in with Google. Events are stored on your PC and shown in your briefings.
  • Spotify, if you connect your Spotify account through your own Spotify developer app.
  • Smart-home devices. Most supported devices (TP-Link Kasa/Tapo, Philips Hue, LIFX, Wemo, Tuya with local keys) are controlled on your local network. Govee control uses Govee’s cloud API with your own key.
  • Feed accounts (Bluesky, Mastodon), if you link your own account.
  • Remote access from your phone over your network or a VPN you set up. Remote access is off until you turn it on and requires a per-install key.

7. GOOGLE API SERVICES — LIMITED USE

Pablo’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Pablo requests read-only access to your calendar, uses it only to show your events and briefings on your own devices, stores it only on your devices, does not transfer it to us or to any third party, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train generalized AI or ML models. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. YOUR RIGHTS (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA AND SIMILAR LAWS)

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of the personal data we hold about you (in practice, your license record and any emails), to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information, we do not use sensitive personal information, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. To exercise a right, email PabloOfflineAI@proton.me from the address used for your purchase; we answer within 30 days (45 days under CCPA). Data on your own devices is under your control: you can view and delete it directly.

International transfers: Stripe and Cloudflare may process license data in the United States and other countries under their own safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

9. SECURITY

Pablo encrypts its databases at rest, keeps sign-in tokens encrypted on your PC, binds sign-in callbacks to your own computer, and requires a per-install key for remote access from your phone. You remain responsible for securing your devices and backing up your data.

10. DELETING YOUR DATA

  • Pablo Desktop: uninstall from Windows Settings. The uninstaller removes the program and its data folders. If anything remains (for example files that were in use), delete the folder you installed into (default Documents\Pablo Desktop) and %LocalAppData%\PabloEnterprise\.
  • Pablo Mobile: uninstall the app; Android deletes its app-private data.
  • License records: email us to request deletion; we keep only what tax law requires.
